# Channel Adaptation

Use this skill after the current master copy is **Audience-ready**. It produces a channel-specific version for a later rules review. It does not approve the copy or validate company rules.

## Required context

Identify the company and campaign. Read the current audience-ready master copy, latest audience diagnosis and review, briefing and intake, channel requirements, company voice, product facts, and applicable configuration. Use the audience evidence to preserve wording that resolved prior concerns and carry forward relevant Minor observations.

Do not adapt a master copy that has unresolved Critical or Material audience findings. If required channel details are missing or a needed fact is absent from the supplied context, request human direction.

Apply company and brand context and sections marked **general** to every campaign. Use only the product, offer, audience, and rule profiles matching the product or offer selected in the briefing. Do not import another profile's terms, eligibility, or required call to action.

## Adapt the message

Follow the requested channel's format, length, action pattern, and call to action defined in the briefing. Preserve the central benefit from the persona-reviewed master copy, factual basis, target, eligibility, and material conditions. Make one offer and one clear action when the channel requires it.

Do not introduce claims, urgency, discounts, availability, eligibility, guarantees, or outcomes that are not supported. Do not omit a material condition in a way that makes the message misleading. When the channel cannot carry every detail, use accurate qualifying language and direct the audience to the destination specified in the briefing for complete conditions, if the briefing and channel support that path. Record material omissions and the rationale; do not imply that a destination exists unless confirmed.

If the channel constraints appear to require a material change to the central promise, do not make that change silently. Return it for master-copy revision and audience review or request the precise human decision needed.

## Write an additive channel artifact

Write `channel-copy.md` for the first adaptation. For a revision, preserve prior artifacts and create `channel-copy-revision-NN.md` using the next sequential number. Include the channel format, copy components, CTA from the briefing, master-copy version used, relevant audience findings addressed or retained, material conditions omitted with rationale, and any unresolved issue for rules review or human input.

Reopen the artifact to verify it follows the requested format and preserves the reviewed message. Stop after channel adaptation; complete the separate rules-review skill next.

## Source attribution

Use source-attributed language: supplied briefing, recorded conditions, and user-provided direction. Describe what the sources state and what each review found. Never infer organizational endorsement of example data, user decisions, or generated content. Human input records information or direction for this run; ask for any facts that remain missing. All outputs remain drafts for human review. Include the demonstration notice specified by the loop prompt outside campaign copy fields.
