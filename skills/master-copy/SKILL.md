# Master Copy

Use this skill after the current intake is **Briefing ready** and the message strategy is **Message strategy ready**. It creates the central campaign message for audience review. It does not conduct persona review, diagnose findings, or write channel copy.

## Required context

Identify the company and campaign. Read the briefing, current intake and message strategy, company/brand/product/rules/audience configuration, current master copy, and latest audience diagnosis or human decision. If audience review has raised Critical or Material findings, use their evidence to guide the revision. If context returned from rules review, include that finding too.

Do not proceed when an upstream decision is not ready, a required source is unreadable, or material facts conflict. Request the specific human decision needed.

Apply company and brand context and sections marked **general** to every campaign. Use only product, offer, audience, and rule profiles matching the product or offer selected in the briefing. Do not carry over another profile's claims, conditions, eligibility, audience, or call to action.

## Create or revise the central message

Create one coherent proposition that expresses the documented strategy. Include:

- A **headline**.
- A **value proposition**.
- A **core creative message**.

Preserve the supplied target, central benefit, factual basis, company voice, and applicable rules. Use confirmed facts only. Do not combine incompatible alternatives or hide an unresolved choice in vague wording.

Do not introduce material product claims, guarantees, urgency, scarcity, eligibility, price, availability, coverage, suitability, financial or family outcomes, or a target change unless supplied context supports them. Do not turn a desired customer outcome into a product guarantee. Master copy is not channel copy: it does not have to meet a channel's length or layout, but it must be truthful and safe for later adaptation.

For a revision, preserve earlier versions and state which Critical or Material findings it addresses. Retain effective elements where possible. If a safe revision would require a missing fact or unresolved business decision, ask a human instead of inventing it.

## Write an additive artifact

Write the initial copy as `master-copy.md`. If one exists, create `master-copy-revision-NN.md` using the next sequential number. Do not modify the briefing, intake, strategy, or earlier copy.

Include company and campaign identifiers, source records and decisions used, confirmed factual and strategic basis, the three copy components, the findings addressed by a revision, any labeled assumption or unresolved concern, and the next allowed action: audience review or human decision. State that master copy has not yet passed audience evaluation.

Reopen the saved artifact to confirm its content and sources. Stop after this record; do not create persona, channel, or rules artifacts.

## Source attribution

Use source-attributed language: supplied briefing, recorded conditions, and user-provided direction. Describe what the sources state and what each review found. Never infer organizational endorsement of example data, user decisions, or generated content. Human input records information or direction for this run; ask for any facts that remain missing. All outputs remain drafts for human review. Include the demonstration notice specified by the loop prompt outside campaign copy fields.
